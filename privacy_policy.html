1. Privacy Policy (Zero Data Collection)

✓ 100% Zero Data Collection & Completely Ad-Free

Smart Portal is committed to protecting your privacy. This application is completely ad-free and does not integrate any third-party commercial advertisements, analytics SDKs, trackers, or marketing profiling libraries.

The application does not collect, record, transmit, sell, or rent any personally identifiable information (such as your name, mobile number, email, precise location history, or unique device identifier) to any external server or third party. All calculations, document editing, and accessibility operations are executed entirely on your local mobile device.

2. Device Permissions & Specific Purposes

Smart Portal requests only the minimum runtime permissions necessary to deliver its assistive utilities and core functions:

A. Location (GPS & Network Provider)

Permission: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Purpose: Used exclusively by the Sathi Guide module to calculate real-time orientation, heading, movement speed, and coordinate data to assist visually impaired and everyday users. This spatial information is processed strictly within device memory in real time and is neither logged nor transmitted remotely.

B. Camera & Flashlight

Permission: CAMERA
Purpose: Utilized by the Divyang Toolkit for convenient flashlight toggling and by the Smart Scanner utility for scanning and digitizing printed documents. The app never captures, stores, or transmits photographic or video content without explicit user action.

C. Microphone & Audio Recording

Permission: RECORD_AUDIO
Purpose: Employed for voice typing (Speech-to-Text) assistance and audio processing features such as the Karaoke Studio. Audio input is handled solely for instantaneous speech-to-text recognition and local audio synthesis. No voice samples are stored or uploaded.

D. Storage, Media & Downloads

Permission: READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
Purpose: Enables users to save generated official letters, PDF documents, and audio recordings directly to the device's public Downloads directory, as well as to select user-specified images or files for document editing.

E. Foreground Service & Media Playback

Permission: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, WAKE_LOCK, POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Purpose: Required to guarantee punctual timetable chime alerts for the School Bell service and uninterrupted background streaming for All India Radio stations when the device screen is switched off or locked. A visible persistent status notification is displayed whenever the background service is running.

Smart Portal serves as an accessible aggregator and utility portal providing convenient entry points to recognized external platforms:

  • Government Portals: Convenient links to publicly accessible educational and departmental services including Shala Darpan, Income Tax Department, Gyan Sankalp Portal, and Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO).
